Voices & setup

The recorded bank adds 20 individual VocalSet singers, with CC BY 4.0 attribution. These voices sing recorded vowels; use the neural sources for arbitrary lyrics. Each source has a neutral baseline you can create, hear, edit and reset. Recorded inputs also support optional offline pitch correction.

This build synthesizes English lyrics with Hoshino Hanami (female soprano, by Lotte V) and TIGER (male, by tigermeat). TIGER includes Fresh and Vinyl vocal modes. Both are downloaded directly from their creators during setup.

Every vocal range has a solo and ensemble option. Choose a male or female voice independently of the section’s range. Section labels describe the musical part; they do not guarantee that a model sounds natural across every register. Dedicated classical soprano, alto, tenor, baritone and bass recordings remain a sound-quality upgrade. You can also use your own recorded/imported voice.

Export your vocal score as MusicXML for an external singing editor, render a voice from the beginning of the project, then import its WAV into that section. If its notes or tempo change, render and import it again.

First setup: run setup-windows.bat, then start-windows.bat. The setup downloads the voicebank directly from its creator (about 1.1 GB total). The prototype voice dependencies include noncommercial terms; use this build for personal prototyping. Commercial use needs suitable licenses or replacement voices. See README and THIRD_PARTY.md.

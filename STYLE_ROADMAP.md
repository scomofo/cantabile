# Vocal styles — expansion plan

Range, voice identity, performance delivery and effects should remain independent. A bass part can use any available voice, but a range selector does not turn a soprano model into an authentic bass. Presets configure controls; specialist models provide new vocal capabilities.

| Style | Useful first addition | Authentic performance gate | Relative difficulty |
|---|---|---|---|
| Barbershop | Tenor / lead / baritone / bass template, coordinated lyrics, little vibrato, restrained room | Matched vowels, synchronized consonants, suitable four-voice blend, chord-aware tuning and listening acceptance | Lowest incremental difficulty for arrangement; convincing blend still needs work |
| Opera | Register-aware presets, expressive pitch/dynamics curves, diction and vibrato shaping | Suitable trained voices for the requested genders, ranges and operatic techniques; phrase/breath behavior; language-specific diction | High for credible operatic sound; modest for editor controls |
| Clean metal | Tight rhythmic phrases, doubles, formant/tone shaping and controlled saturation | Strong clean/belted source performances that retain lyric clarity in a dense mix | Moderate with suitable voices |
| Harsh metal | Separate clean/harsh lanes, transitions and vocal effects | Dedicated growl/scream models or recordings, intelligible consonants and stable clean-to-harsh transitions | High; distortion alone is insufficient |
| Hip-hop | Existing syllable steps, accents, swing, holds, retriggers and effects | More detailed timing and pitch inflections, breaths, spoken/rapped training material, consistent pronunciation | Sequencing works now; natural rap remains a voice/performance upgrade |

Recommended next slice: listen to the supplied demos and test the microphone on the target PC, then add a barbershop arrangement template and a reusable voice registry. The registry should describe supported languages, useful pitch range, singing techniques, phoneme inventory, inference adapter and license. Extend it with auditioned opera/metal voices without changing score editing.

Use recorded/imported vocal stems now when an authentic performance is needed outside the installed models' capabilities. The app can mix and process those recordings, but does not synthesize new words in their voices or clone a microphone recording.

Success is a listening result, not a preset name: clear requested words, plausible register, stable transitions, appropriate phrasing, and a convincing ensemble or double. Record exact part/word/time issues before increasing the model palette.
